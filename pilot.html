The logistics network

Delivery that runs itself — from pickup to your door

When an order is placed on Seli Market, a delivery job is created. The nearest available pilot accepts, picks up the package with a secure code, and takes it straight to the recipient — every step tracked live.

For merchants it removes the headache of logistics and chases. For pilots it turns spare time into a reliable income stream.

How delivery works

A secure, trackable journey for every package.

1

Create a delivery job

When a merchant ships an order, a delivery job is created with pickup and delivery details automatically.

2

A pilot accepts

Verified pilots near the pickup point accept the job in-app — no more waiting on couriers.

3

Secure pickup

The pilot confirms pickup with a unique code, then the package moves — tracked live all the way.

4

Handover & proof

Delivery is confirmed with another code plus photo or signature proof, then the pilot is paid.
