Everything in one marketplace

The main features that power Seli Market, end to end.

Video-first shopping

Products come alive in short videos. Watch a product in action, then buy in one tap — no leaving the feed.

Escrow buyer protection

Every payment is held in escrow. If no dispute is raised, funds are automatically released to the seller 24 hours after delivery confirmation.

Shop-to-Earn rewards

Turn purchases and video reviews into shopping credits that keep your money working harder every time.

Verified merchants

Every store is vetted. Buyers see a verified badge and real reviews, so they trust who they're buying from.

Shemi AI assistant

Upload a product photo and Shemi writes the title, description, and tags — or generates professional product photos.

Pilot delivery network

A dedicated logistics network of verified delivery agents ships orders fast, with live tracking from pickup to door.
