Business model

How Seli makes money

Revenue streams wired directly into the marketplace loop - matched to the value Seli creates for sellers and buyers.

Vendor commission - a flat 5.5% on every order, collected when the escrow releases.
Delivery fees - per-order fees from the Seli Pilot network for pickup, tracking and handover.
Seller tools - premium tiers with storefront analytics, ads and priority support as merchants scale.
Auto payouts in 24H - fast settlement keeps sellers liquid and choosing Seli first.
