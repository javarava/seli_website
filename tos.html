2. Who we are & the Services

Seli operates a video-first social commerce platform in Nigeria. Through the Services we provide a range of connected products:

  • Seli Market - where buyers watch curated videos and photo carousels, discover products, place orders and pay, and where sellers open verified stores, list products and receive payouts;
  • Seli Pilot - an on-demand delivery network that connects orders to verified delivery agents, with live tracking and code-secured handovers;

The contract for the sale of a product is between the buyer and the seller; Seli provides the platform, escrow and delivery rails that make the transaction safe.
