Why join Seli

Work where your work is seen

Every role here directly shapes what millions of people will experience - starting with Lagos.

Ship fast

You own work from idea to launch. Small features ship in days, not months - and you see the impact immediately.

Own it

No layers. You decide the approach, pick the tools, and answer for the outcome. Real ownership from day one.

Grow with the platform

Early on a marketplace means broad scope. You'll stretch across problems most people only get to specialise in years later.

Do work that matters

Digital commerce in Nigeria is still early. Everything we build helps real sellers and buyers transact safely and grow.

What the process looks like

Straightforward, respectful of your time, and mostly over video calls.

Apply
