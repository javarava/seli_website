How we handle your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Seli collects when you use the Seli app, website, Seli Pilot delivery network and Shemi AI, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated: September 14, 2026  ·  Applies to: buyers, sellers (merchants), pilots, guests and website visitors

1. Overview & who we are

Seli Trading Company ("Seli", "we", "us", or "our") is a Nigerian company operating a video-first social commerce platform that lets people discover products, buy and sell, pay through escrow-protected transactions, earn rewards, and grow a verified store — supported by Seli's on-demand delivery network ("Seli Pilot") and an AI listing assistant ("Shemi AI").

This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand what personal data we process when you use the Seli mobile application, the seli.com.ng website, and any related products, features, tooling or services that link to this policy (together, the "Services"). It also explains the choices you can make about your information.

We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), and — where you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom — the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR. We are committed to fair, lawful and transparent processing, and to protecting the information you trust us with.

The terms "buyer", "seller" / "merchant", and "pilot" refer to the different roles users can take on within the Services. If you use the Services in more than one role (for example, you both shop and sell), this policy applies to how we process your information in each role.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors to the seli.com.ng website and anyone who browses public content;
  • Buyers who create accounts, browse the video feed, place orders, pay and receive deliveries;
  • Sellers / merchants who open stores, list products, publish videos or photo carousels, process orders and receive payouts;
  • Pilots who register with the Seli Pilot network to accept and complete delivery jobs;
  • Anyone who contacts our support team, participates in surveys or contests, or otherwise engages with the Services.

This policy does not cover how third parties (such as payment processors, banks, or other websites and services you visit through links on Seli) process your data. Those parties have their own privacy policies. Where we share information with a third party to provide a service you requested, you should also review that party's privacy practices.

Summary: Anything you do on Seli that relates to your personal information is governed by this policy. When you leave Seli or transact with a third-party processor, that party's own policy applies instead.

3. Information we collect

The information we process depends on how you use the Services. We collect only what we need to provide the features you use. The categories below describe what we collect and why.

3.1 Account and profile information

When you create an account or update your profile, we collect:

  • Full name (or business name / store name for sellers);
  • Email address and a phone number that can receive SMS verification codes;
  • A password or other authentication credential (stored as an irreversible hash);
  • Profile photo, avatar, and store bio or store description;
  • Any links you add to your profile (for example a website or social media handle);
  • Language and in-app preferences;
  • Whether you are registering as a buyer, seller, pilot, or a combination of roles.

3.2 Identity and verification (KYC) information

To keep the platform trustworthy, some activities require identity verification. When you open a store, register as a pilot, or perform certain high-value or high-risk transactions we may collect (with your consent):

  • Government-issued identification numbers (such as your National Identification Number, NIN) and images of identity documents;
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN) or other verification identifiers, where required to prevent fraud or to enable payments;
  • Date of birth and age verification;
  • Proof of address (for example a utility bill), delivery zone and operating areas;
  • Bank account details used for payouts, including account name, number and bank;
  • Proof of purchase or proof of identity for sellers of specific regulated or pre-owned categories;
  • For pilots: driver's licence, vehicle details, means of identification, character references, and any other information required to vet delivery agents.

Verification data is processed by us and by trusted third-party identity-verification providers. Some of this data is used solely to confirm your identity, not to build a browsing profile.

3.3 Payment and financial information

When you buy, sell, withdraw funds or use Seli rewards, we collect and process:

  • Transaction records: amounts, order references, escrow status, delivery confirmation timestamps, refunds and chargebacks;
  • Payment method information. Card numbers are collected and stored by our PCI-DSS compliant payment processors (for example Paystack); we do not store full card details ourselves;
  • Wallet balances, reward credits, Shop-to-Earn points, and payout history;
  • Tax information where required (for example a Tax Identification Number, TIN) for sellers eligible for proceeds or where withholding applies;
  • Bank account details for automatic 24-hour payouts.

3.4 Orders, listings and marketplace activity

To operate the marketplace we collect information generated by your activity:

  • For sellers: product photos, videos and descriptions, listings, prices, inventory, sales and payout reports, store performance analytics, and reviews you receive;
  • For buyers: carts and wishlists, order history, favourite stores, search history, video watch history, product views, and reviews you leave;
  • Delivery addresses (home, office, pickup points) you save for deliveries;
  • Communications between buyers, sellers and pilots conducted through in-app chat or notes attached to orders (for example pickup instructions);
  • Buyers Claims and dispute history, including evidence you submit.

3.5 Delivery and location information (Seli Pilot)

Seli Pilot matches orders to delivery agents and needs location data to work:

  • Sellers and buyers: the pickup and delivery addresses you provide, and optional permission-based precise location to suggest nearby drop-off/pickup points;
  • Pilots: we collect precise real-time GPS location only while a delivery job is active and with your permission, so that orders can be tracked and routed. This can include a journey history for the jobs you completed;
  • Delivery metadata: delivery codes, handover confirmations, timestamps, and proof-of-delivery photos (if captured), which may be shared with the buyer and seller for the order.

You can revoke location permission at any time through your device settings. If you do, some delivery features (such as live tracking of your job) may not work correctly. We do not collect background location without your permission, and pilots' GPS is active only during assigned jobs.

3.6 Shemi AI inputs and outputs

When you use Shemi AI to generate listings:

  • We receive the product photo(s) you upload and the instructions you give (for example "write in pidgin-English");
  • We generate titles, descriptions, tags, category suggestions and images, which we associate with your account so you can review, edit and apply them to a listing;
  • Shemi output that you apply to a public listing becomes part of the listing you publish and is therefore public;
  • We may keep prompts and outputs for product-improvement and safety purposes (see Section 6 and the retention terms in Section 10). You can request deletion via privacy@seli.com.ng.

3.7 Communications and support

  • Messages you exchange with our support team via email, in-app chat or social media;
  • Recordings or transcripts of calls where permitted by law (we will tell you when a call is recorded);
  • Feedback, survey responses, contest entries and testimonials you send us;
  • Copies of documents you provide to establish your identity when raising a dispute.

3.8 Device, usage and technical information

When you use the Services, we automatically receive technical information about the device and your interaction with us:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising IDs and device IDs), device model, operating system and app version;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Log data: pages and screens visited, taps, feed impressions, watch time, feature usage, crash logs, network diagnostics and app errors;
  • Browser type, language and referring URLs on the website;
  • Dates and times of your sessions.

3.9 Cookies and similar technologies

The seli.com.ng website and in-app web views use cookies, pixels, local storage and similar technologies to:

  • Essential: keep you signed in, remember security preferences, prevent fraud and keep the Services secure;
  • Performance & analytics: understand how pages are used, measure errors and improve the experience;
  • Personalisation & advertising: with your consent where required, remember preferences and measure the effectiveness of our campaigns.

You can control cookies through your browser or device settings, but disabling essential cookies may break parts of the Services.

4. How we collect information

  • Information you provide directly — when you register, complete a profile, list a product, place an order, chat with support, or enter an address or payment detail.
  • Information generated by your use — orders, listings, views, searches, watch history, delivery confirmations, rewards earned and reviews.
  • Information from devices automatically — app logs, usage analytics, crash reports and device/location data described in Sections 3.5 and 3.8.
  • Information from third parties — verification providers (identity and BVN checks), payment processors (transaction status), analytics providers, and — with your consent — social or outreach providers you authorise.
  • Public sources — where you link a public profile or where legally permitted, to verify merchant or pilot credentials.

If you provide us with information about another person (for example, a recipient's delivery address), you confirm that you have the right to share that information and, where appropriate, that they are aware of this policy.

5. Legal bases for processing

Under applicable data protection law (notably the NDPA/NDPR in Nigeria and the GDPR/UK GDPR where they apply to you), we must have a lawful basis to process personal data. We rely on the following bases:

BasisWhen we rely on it
Performance of a contract To provide the Services you asked for: processing orders, running escrow, matching and tracking pilot deliveries, issuing payouts, and delivering account features.
Consent Where we ask your permission first — for example precise GPS during pilot jobs, optional marketing, cookie personalisation, or processing special-category/verification data in certain cases.
Compliance with legal obligations Where the law requires it: anti-money-laundering and KYC obligations, tax reporting, records required by regulators, and responding to lawful requests from authorities.
Legitimate interests For things like keeping the platform safe and free of fraud, detecting and stopping unauthorised activity, product analytics and improvement, customer support, network security and business continuity — balanced against your rights and expectations.
Vital interests In rare, time-critical situations where we must protect someone's life or physical safety (for example a pilot or delivery safety incident).

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before your withdrawal.

Jurisdiction note: Different bases may apply to the same data for different purposes, and a single set of data (for example your phone number) can be processed under multiple bases. If you have questions about the legal basis for a specific processing activity, contact privacy@seli.com.ng.

6. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to operate the Services and improve them, including to:

  • Create and manage accounts — registration, authentication, profile setup and account recovery;
  • Provide the marketplace — showing the video feed and search results, letting you create and manage stores, listings and product pages, and processing orders from purchase to delivery confirmation;
  • Run escrow and payments — holding buyer funds, releasing payments to sellers on delivery confirmation, processing refunds, chargebacks and Buyers Claims, and paying pilots;
  • Operate Seli Pilot — verifying pilots, matching delivery jobs, live tracking, delivery codes and proof of delivery;
  • Power Shemi AI — generating the titles, descriptions, tags, categories and images you request and applying them to your listings;
  • Verify identity and prevent fraud — KYC, device and account risk checks, detecting fake reviews, bots, collusive fraud and unauthorised access;
  • Provide customer support — answering questions, resolving disputes and handling complaints, including through call recordings where permitted;
  • Run rewards and loyalty — awarding, tracking and redeeming Shop-to-Earn points and other promotions;
  • Personalise the experience — recommending products, merchants and content based on your interests, language and usage;
  • Communicate with you — order updates, delivery tracking, payout confirmations, security alerts, and (with your consent) marketing and news;
  • Measure and improve — analysing usage patterns, running A/B tests, fixing bugs, and improving quality, safety and performance;
  • Ensure safety and compliance — enforcing our Terms, blocking prohibited products and harmful behaviour, and meeting legal and regulatory obligations;
  • Protect our rights — establishing, exercising or defending legal claims, and managing audits and corporate governance.

Shemi AI outputs are your content: we do not use your product photos, prompts or generated listings to train models used to generate content for other users. Where we improve Shemi's quality using aggregated, de-identified data, it is stripped of identities before use.

7. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the categories of recipients below, and only to the extent needed to provide or protect the Services:

CategoryWhy we shareExamples of data
Other users To connect buyers, sellers and pilots for transactions and delivery. Your name, store name, phone number, delivery address, order and listing details, and reviews — shared with the other party to your transaction.
Payment processors To collect payments, release escrow and make auto payouts. Transaction amounts, escrow status, delivery-confirmation timestamps, bank/payout details. Card details are handled by the processor, not by us.
Identity & verification providers For KYC, BVN checks and fraud prevention during seller/pilot vetting. Name, NIN/BVN, identity documents, date of birth, address, phone.
Cloud, hosting & infrastructure providers To store data and operate the app, databases, media, notifications and email. Account, listing, order and log data hosted on their infrastructure.
Analytics & product-safety providers To measure usage, detect bugs and block abuse. Aggregated usage metrics, device/OS info, crash reports, and anomaly signals.
Customer-support & communications tools To answer tickets and send transactional messages (SMS/intents, email, VoIP). Contact details, order references and support conversation content.
Professional advisers & auditors For legal, accounting, insurance and audit purposes. Only that data relevant to the engagement, under confidentiality obligations.
Regulators & law enforcement Where we are legally obliged or to protect rights and safety. Only what the lawful request requires.
Buyers/new owners in a business transfer In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (Section 16). Personal data supporting the transferred business, under this policy.

We require all service providers that receive personal data on our behalf to process it under our instructions, keep it confidential, and apply appropriate security measures.

8. International data transfers

Our primary operations and data storage are in Nigeria. Some of our infrastructure providers, payment processors and analytics services store or process data in other countries (for example data centres in Europe, the United States or other regions).

Where your personal data is transferred out of Nigeria, we rely on legal frameworks that provide suitable protections, including:

  • Transfers to countries recognised as providing an adequate level of protection;
  • Standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards approved by the relevant regulator; and
  • Binding corporate rules or processor agreements where applicable.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you can request a copy of the safeguards we use to protect your data when it is transferred internationally by contacting privacy@seli.com.ng.

9. Marketing & your choices

Transactional vs. marketing communications

  • Transactional messages (order updates, delivery tracking, payslip confirmations, security alerts, policy changes) are necessary to use the Services and are not promotional. You may not be able to opt out entirely, but you can choose how you receive them in account settings.
  • Marketing messages (newsletters, offers, creator campaigns) are sent only with your consent or where permitted by law. You can opt out at any time via the unsubscribe link in any message or in your account/app notification settings.

In-app and device-level controls

You can control push notifications, SMS, email, and location permissions from your device settings and within the app. Changing a device setting does not affect data already processed under this policy, but it will stop future collection/use of the affected information.

Advertising

Where we run advertising on or off the platform and rely on personalised ads, we obtain consent where required and respect your advertising-ID and cookie choices. Opting out does not stop ads — it stops personalised targeting and use of your data for that purpose.

10. How long we keep your information

We keep personal data for no longer than is necessary for the purposes described in this policy, taking into account legal, regulatory, tax, accounting and fraud-prevention requirements. Typical time frames are:

DataTypical retentionNotes
Account & profile Until you delete your account Schedules deletion started automatically once you request it (Section 13).
Order, transaction & payout records Up to 7 years after the transaction, then erased or anonymised Longer where required by tax or financial regulations, or where a dispute/claim is ongoing.
KYC & verification data Up to 5 years after verification or account closure Kept to comply with AML obligations and prevent future fraud.
Delivery & location records (pilots) Up to 90 days after each active job Longer where needed to resolve an ongoing dispute or incident.
Shemi AI prompts & outputs Until you delete them or your account closes Logs may be kept in de-identified form for safety and quality.
Usage & crash logs 30–90 days Then aggregated/de-identified.
Marketing preferences & opt-out records As long as needed to honour your choice E.g. suppression lists are kept even after account closure.
Support chats & call recordings Up to 3 years from last contact Longer if referenced by a legal proceeding.

When retention periods pass we securely delete or irreversibly anonymise the data, so it can no longer identify you. Legal hold orders suspend deletion until the hold is lifted.

11. Children & minors

The Services are not directed at children. You must be at least 18 years old to create a Seli account, open a store, or register as a pilot. Where local law sets a higher age (for example 16 or 18) for the data subject, we apply the higher threshold.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without the consent of a parent or guardian, contact us at privacy@seli.com.ng and we will take steps to remove the information.

12. How we protect your data

We use technical, organisational and administrative safeguards appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) for all web and API traffic, and encryption at rest for stored personal data;
  • Hashed credentials — passwords are stored as salted hashes and never in plain text;
  • Access controls — least-privilege access to production data, with role-based permissions, rotation of credentials and logging of access;
  • Fraud & abuse detection — automated checks on accounts, devices and transactions;
  • Vetting of staff & providers who hold confidentiality and security obligations;
  • Security testing — regular reviews, penetration testing and dependency scanning;
  • Incident response — a defined breach-handling plan. Where a breach creates a real risk to your rights, we notify you and the relevant regulator (for example the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, NDPC) as required by law.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please help protect your account by using a strong, unique password, enabling any offered two-factor authentication, and never sharing your delivery codes or verification codes with anyone.

13. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live and the law that applies to you, you may have the following rights over your personal data:

RightWhat it lets you do
AccessReceive a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.
RectificationCorrect inaccurate or incomplete information (for example update your phone number or store address).
ErasureAsk that we delete your data where the legal conditions are met. This is not absolute — some data must be kept for legal or transactional reasons.
RestrictionAsk us to pause processing of specified data while a dispute about it is resolved.
PortabilityReceive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format and (where technically feasible) transmit it to another service.
ObjectionObject to processing based on legitimate interests, including analytics, and to direct marketing.
Withdraw consentWhere processing relies on consent (e.g. precise location, marketing), withdraw it at any time without penalty.
ComplaintLodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or your local supervisory authority.

How to exercise your rights

  • Use the in-app account, privacy and notification settings to update or export many types of data directly;
  • Email privacy@seli.com.ng for anything else (including account deletion requests and data copies);
  • We verify requests to protect you — we may ask you to confirm your identity (for example entering a one-time code sent to your registered phone) before actioning sensitive requests;
  • We aim to respond within 30 days; if a request is complex or voluminous we may take up to the period allowed by law and will tell you if so. Requests that are unfounded or repetitive may carry a reasonable administrative fee where law permits.

Account deletion: deleting your account closes your access and starts deletion of your account/profile data. Transaction, payout and KYC records required by law or for fraud prevention are retained in line with Section 10 and not removed by account deletion.

14. Automated decision-making

We use automated systems in a few areas. Where a decision is made solely by automation and has a legal or similarly significant effect on you, we provide a way to request human review and to contest the decision:

  • Account & transaction risk checks — automated scoring used to detect fraud, money laundering or other abuse (for example declining a suspicious order or placing a hold on a payout). These decisions materially support our legal and legitimate-security obligations;
  • Identity verification — automated comparisons between your submitted document, selfie and registered details. You can always complete verification with a human agent instead;
  • Shemi AI content generation — AI-generated titles, tags and images are suggestions that you review, edit and approve before they are applied. They are never published automatically without your action.

If you believe an automated decision has affected you unfairly, email privacy@seli.com.ng with details and we will arrange a manual review.

15. Third-party links & services

The Services may contain links to third-party websites, stores, payment pages, verification portals, or app-store listings. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. Once you leave Seli, the third party's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Communications between buyers, sellers and pilots that happen outside Seli (for example direct WhatsApp or phone calls) are not covered by this policy — we encourage you to keep transaction conversations inside the app where possible.

16. Business transfers

If Seli is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganisation, financing, or the sale of all or part of our business, your personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. Before any such transfer where permitted by law, we will provide appropriate notice via the app, website or email, and the recipient must continue to process your data in line with this policy (or a materially similar, applicable one) and applicable law.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or business practices. When we do:

  • We update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and publish a version history summary;
  • For material changes, we give you prominent notice in the app, on the website, or by email at least so you have reasonable time to review them before they take effect;
  • Continuing to use the Services after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy. For changes that require consent, we will ask for it separately where law requires.

18. Contact us & the Data Protection Officer

Questions, requests and complaints about this Privacy Policy or our data practices are handled by our data protection team. You can reach us at:

  • Data privacy: privacy@seli.com.ng
  • Customer support: support@seli.com.ng
  • Contact page: seli.com.ng/contact.html
  • Postal address: Seli Trading Company, Lagos, Nigeria (for postal correspondence, write to our registered office in Lagos — address confirmed via privacy@seli.com.ng).

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handled a privacy matter, you may contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) — the regulator responsible for enforcing the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 — or your local data-protection supervisory authority. For individuals in the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your national supervisory authority.

Please note: for account-deletion or data-rights requests, contacting privacy@seli.com.ng is the fastest way to reach the right team. Support requests about orders or deliveries are handled faster through the in-app support chat or support@seli.com.ng.
