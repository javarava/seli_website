Legal · Escrow & Buyer Protection

Your money is safe until you're happy.

This Escrow Policy explains how Seli protects buyer payments. Funds you pay are held securely and released to the seller only after your order is delivered. If no dispute is raised, escrow funds are automatically released 24 hours after delivery confirmation - and this policy explains how Seli Buyer Protection handles things when an order goes wrong.

Last updated: September 14,  ·  Applies to: buyers, sellers (merchants) and delivery agents using escrow-protected transactions

1. What escrow is

When you pay for an order on Seli, your money is not sent straight to the seller. Instead, it is held in escrow by Seli - a safeguarded balance that sits between buyer and seller until the order is completed. This is the foundation of the "100% Buyer Protection" promise you see on our marketing pages.

Escrow works in three steps:

1

You pay

Your payment is captured and held in escrow - the seller can see the order is funded but cannot yet touch the money.

2

Delivery confirmed

Your order is delivered and you confirm completion with your delivery code, or the 24-hour Buyer Claims window from delivery confirmation closes without a dispute.

3

Funds released

Escrow automatically releases the amount to the seller, net of fees, 24 hours after delivery confirmation if no dispute is raised. Buyer funds are only ever released on the conditions in this policy.

Important: Seli holds escrowed funds as a payment-protection mechanism. Escrow is not a bank deposit: it does not earn interest and is not covered by any deposit insurance scheme.

2. How escrow works

2.1 From payment to release

StageWhat happensWho can access the funds
1. Order placed & paid Successful payment moves the order value into escrow; the order becomes "funded" and is sent for fulfilment and pickup. Neither party can withdraw. Seli holds the funds.
2. Picked up & delivered The seller prepares the order, Seli Pilot delivers it, and the buyer confirms handover with their delivery code. Still held in escrow.
3. 24-hour protection window A 24-hour Buyer Claims window runs from delivery confirmation (shown on your order) during which the buyer can raise a dispute. Still held in escrow; a dispute pauses auto-release.
4. Auto-release If no dispute is raised in that 24 hours (or one is resolved in the seller's favour), escrow automatically releases the order value to the seller, minus fees. Seller receives proceeds; buyer's payment is complete.

2.2 Where escrowed funds sit

Escrowed amounts are recorded on your Seli wallet and held by Seli (or our authorised payment partners) in segregated operational accounts. Card payments sit with the acquiring processor until the transaction settles; the escrow envelope we describe is the commercial hold we manage on top of that settlement.

2.3 Seller payouts

Once released, net proceeds are paid out automatically, generally within 24 hours, to the bank account linked to the seller's profile. If payout fails, the amount sits in the seller's wallet until valid details are provided.

3. What Buyer Protection covers

Seli Buyer Protection covers orders paid through escrow when a verifiable problem occurs. The covered events are:

  • Non-delivery - you paid but the order was never delivered, or the pickup/delivery could not be completed through no fault of yours;
  • Wrong item - you received a product that is materially different from its listing (different item, wrong size, wrong colour, wrong quantity);
  • Obvious damage - the product arrived damaged or its packaging was visibly broken such that the product could not be used as described;
  • Material misrepresentation - the listing description, photos or videos gave a substantially wrong impression of the item (for example condition, origin or authenticity labels);
  • Unauthorised transaction - a charge on your account that you did not authorise and that resulted from misuse of your account by another person (reported promptly).

For a claim to succeed, you must demonstrate the covered event with credible evidence - usually order history, tracking/confirmation, and photos or videos of what you received.

4. What Buyer Protection does not cover

Buyer Protection is not a general warranty, insurance policy or money-back guarantee. Claims will not succeed where the issue is, for example:

  • Buyer's remorse or a change of mind after successful delivery;
  • Post-delivery product performance issues that are ordinary use (wear and tear, battery life, perishable shelf-life), unless the listing warranted otherwise;
  • Damage caused after the delivery code was confirmed and the package released to you;
  • Delivery to an incorrect address or an unavailable recipient provided by you;
  • An order you cancelled after the seller had already purchased stock or was executing fulfilment, or an order you abandoned after delivery was arranged;
  • Claims raised against an order that was not paid via escrow (for example an off-platform or cash-on-delivery transaction);
  • Fraudulent, abusive or repeated claims designed to obtain free products;
  • Failure to preserve and return the product when return is required under the resolution (Section 6);
  • Seller conduct governed by the merchant's own disclosed return policy where that policy is consistent with these Terms.
Good-faith use: Buyer Protection exists to protect genuine buyers. If we detect claim fraud or collusion, we may reject claims, close accounts, and where appropriate report the conduct to law enforcement.

5. Raising a Buyer Claim

  • When: within 24 hours of delivery confirmation - the Buyer Claims window shown on your order, which is why escrow auto-releases after that window. For non-delivery, you can raise a claim as soon as the tracking shows no progress past the expected delivery time;
  • Where: from the order details page in the Seli app (Support → Orders → "Raise a claim"), or by contacting support@seli.com.ng;
  • What you'll provide: the order reference, a description of the problem, and supporting evidence - clear photos or a short video of the item and packaging, delivery notes, or messages with the seller/pilot;
  • Pause on escrow: raising a claim pauses any pending release so funds stay where they are while we investigate;
  • Responding to the seller: the seller is given the chance to respond with their own evidence (e.g. packing video, proof of pickup, delivery logs) before we decide.

You must raise the claim in good faith and provide accurate information. If you later receive, find, or are refunded for the same item in a way that duplicates a refund, you must let us know.

6. How claims are resolved

We review each claim on the evidence from both sides and the order/tracking record. The possible outcomes are:

OutcomeWhat happensMoney
Full refund Claim upheld - the order failed materially. We refund the order value to your wallet or original payment method. Escrow returned to you (or a refund charge raised). Seller doesn't get paid for the order.
Partial refund Claim partially upheld (e.g. some items of the order were correct, or the deficit is small). A portion is refunded; the rest releases to the seller.
Return & refund We arrange (where practical) for you to return the item, and refund once a drop-off or pickup is completed. Refund on completed return; return cost borne per the resolution.
Replacement Where the seller agrees, we keep escrow and the seller re-fills the order. Funds stay in escrow until the replacement is delivered.
Claim rejected Evidence does not support the claim, or the issue falls outside Section 3. Escrow auto-releases to the seller after the 24-hour window closes (no dispute raised).

Refund method & timing

  • Wallet refunds show immediately and can be used for your next purchase;
  • Refunds to a card can take a few working days depending on the issuing bank;
  • We aim to complete case reviews within 5–10 working days. Complex or high-value cases can take longer, and we will keep you updated.

7. Release of funds to sellers

Unless a dispute pauses release or an investigation is underway, escrow funds are automatically released 24 hours after delivery confirmation if no dispute is raised. You never have to manually "confirm release" - the buyer's delivery code confirms the handover and the 24-hour window keeps the money safe while any issue can still be raised.

Escrow release vs payout: "Release" means escrow moving the order value into the seller's Seli wallet - this happens automatically 24 hours after delivery confirmation if no dispute is raised. "Payout" means transferring that wallet balance to the seller's linked bank account, paid out automatically once released, generally within 24 hours.

Sellers receive escrow funds only when an order qualifies for release:

  • The buyer confirmed the delivery with their delivery code and the 24-hour Buyer Claims window passed without a dispute - release happens automatically at the end of that window; or
  • A dispute was resolved in the seller's favour (Section 6);
  • Where delivery could not be confirmed (for example an unattempted delivery), we may release funds after a defined investigation period if tracking confirms delivery and no dispute was filed.

Released proceeds are subject to the seller's commission of 5.5% and any applicable fees (see the Terms of Service, Section 9) and are paid automatically, generally within 24 hours.

If a seller's payout would be fraudulent, collusive or reverse the effect of a cash-out scam, we may hold or reverse it in line with Sections 8–9 below and notify the seller.

8. Chargebacks & reversals

If a buyer, the buyer's bank, or a payment provider initiates a chargeback or reversal on a funded order, we will:

  • Investigate the order and participate in the chargeback process with the payment provider, including submitting delivery and claim evidence where we have it;
  • Deduct the reversed amount from the applicable escrow, refunds, or the seller's proceeds where the reversal relates to an order that had already been paid out;
  • If amounts cannot be deducted in full, we may suspend payouts and, where material, pursue recovery in line with the Terms of Service (set-off rights).

Sellers whose orders are repeatedly reversed may lose eligibility for fast payouts and have their store account reviewed.

9. Determinations & disputes

  • Our resolution of a Buyer Claim is based on the evidence available and this policy;
  • If a buyer and seller agree on an outcome among themselves (for example a mutual cancellation), we implement it where it complies with this policy;
  • If you disagree with a determination, contact support@seli.com.ng within 14 days with new evidence, and we will review;
  • Where a claim crosses fraud, criminal or regulatory territory, we may refer it to the appropriate authority and withhold funds pending their direction;
  • Except where prohibited by law, our determination is final and binding between the parties, subject to your rights under the dispute-resolution section of the Terms of Service (Section 19).
Act fast: for non-delivery or card issues, raising your claim early improves the evidence available (tracking, GPS logs, delivery codes) and shortens resolution time.
